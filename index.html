CS Futures in the Age of AI

A seminar series on how AI is changing research in computer science.

We can't stop thinking about AI, and for good reason: it's affecting everything we do.

To contribute further to the discussion, the CS AI Council is hosting a new seminar series, CS Futures in the Age of AI. Members of research groups across the department will talk about the impact AI is having on their research areas, and how they think their fields may evolve in this new environment.

These talks should be of broad interest to everyone curious about the impacts of AI on CS research, from a variety of perspectives. They particularly target graduate students and young researchers who are charting their courses in this brave new age.

Where: WARF Seminar Room, 7th Floor, Morgridge Hall
When: Times vary. See each talk below.
